# Code of conduct

Mandala adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in this repository, the other maktub-ai repositories, and any Mandala community space.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately by email at the address on the [maktub-ai organization profile](https://github.com/maktub-ai). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants who break these rules.
